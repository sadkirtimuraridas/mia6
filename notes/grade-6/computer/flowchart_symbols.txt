FLOWCHART SYMBOLS

1. Terminal / Terminator
   Shape: Oval (Ellipse)
   Purpose: Indicates the Start or Stop/End of a flowchart.

2. Input / Output
   Shape: Parallelogram
   Purpose: Used to enter data or display results.

3. Process
   Shape: Rectangle
   Purpose: Represents an action, instruction, or calculation.

4. Decision
   Shape: Diamond
   Purpose: Represents a condition with branches, such as Yes/No or True/False.

5. On-page connector
   Shape: Circle
   Purpose: Connects different parts of a flowchart on the same page.

6. Off-page connector
   Shape: Pentagon (usually home-plate shaped)
   Purpose: Indicates that the flowchart continues on another page.

7. Flowline / Flow line
   Shape: Line with an arrowhead
   Purpose: Shows the direction and sequence of steps.
