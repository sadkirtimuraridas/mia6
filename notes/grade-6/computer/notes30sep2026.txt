CHAPTER 9
ALGORITHM

Algorithm – It is a step-by-step set of instructions used to solve a problem or perform a task.

Characteristics of Algorithms
Precision – Every step should be precisely defined.
Uniqueness – Every step should be uniquely defined and unambiguous.
Finiteness – An algorithm should have a finite number of steps.
Well-defined Input – The input should be clearly defined.
Well-defined Output – The output should be clearly defined.
Effectiveness – The algorithm should be efficient in terms of time and space.
Algorithm to Calculate the Sum of Two Numbers

Step 1 – Start
Step 2 – Read two numbers and store them in A and B.
Step 3 – Calculate A + B.
Step 4 – Print the result as the sum.
Step 5 – Stop
